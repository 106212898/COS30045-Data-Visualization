Where household energy goes

Across Australian homes, heating and cooling typically make up the largest share of electricity use, followed by hot water and refrigeration. Entertainment appliances — televisions, gaming consoles, and sound systems — draw a smaller but steadily growing slice, driven by larger screens and always-on standby modes.

Energy Rating labels, introduced under the national Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (GEMS) scheme, give households a standardised way to compare appliances before buying. The more stars on the label, the less energy the appliance is expected to use for the same size and features.

Quick tips

Cutting appliance running costs

Switch appliances off at the wall rather than leaving them on standby overnight.

Check the Energy Rating label and compare star ratings, not just price, when buying new appliances.

Group entertainment devices on a single powerboard so they're easy to switch off together.
